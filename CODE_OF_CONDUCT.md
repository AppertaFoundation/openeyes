# OpenEyes Contributor Code of Conduct

## Purpose

OpenEyes is an open-source clinical software project stewarded by the Apperta Foundation as custodian. We are committed to providing a professional, respectful and safe environment for everyone contributing to the project.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to participation in the OpenEyes contributor community, including the Apperta Accredited Contributor programme, project repositories, meetings and other project communication channels.

Clinical use of OpenEyes is governed separately by the healthcare organisations deploying the software and their Accredited Professional Services Partners.

## Expected behaviour

Contributors are expected to:

- communicate professionally and respectfully;
- respect differing views, experience and backgrounds;
- provide constructive feedback;
- respect the privacy and confidentiality of others;
- raise disagreements or concerns without personal criticism or intimidation.

The following behaviour is not acceptable:

- harassment, bullying, intimidation or threats;
- discriminatory, abusive or derogatory comments;
- unwanted sexual attention or sexualised content;
- deliberate disruption or persistent hostile behaviour;
- publishing another person's private information without permission;
- retaliation against anyone who raises a concern in good faith.

## Reporting concerns

Concerns about behaviour within the OpenEyes contributor community may be reported privately to **openeyes@apperta.org**.

Apperta, as custodian, will review concerns and take proportionate action where necessary. This may include a warning, restriction or removal of contributor access, suspension or removal from the Accredited Contributor programme, or referral to another appropriate organisation or authority where required.

Reports will be handled sensitively and information will only be shared where reasonably necessary to investigate or address the concern.

## Contributor eligibility and underage users

The Accredited Contributor programme is limited to individuals who have the **legal capacity, under applicable local law, to enter into the relevant Contributor Agreement**.

OpenEyes does not provide community or social functionality directed at children or other underage users.

## Contributor governance

Formal contributions to OpenEyes are managed through Apperta's **Accredited Contributor programme** and associated Contributor Agreements. This Code of Conduct complements those arrangements and does not create a support or service-level agreement for the OpenEyes software.

## Responsibility

Apperta Foundation CIC, as custodian of OpenEyes, is responsible for maintaining and applying this Code of Conduct.
